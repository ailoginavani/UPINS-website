About UPINS

Up India Solutions

UPINS Private Limited is an engineering-driven company delivering advanced solutions across industrial logistics, container manufacturing, and aerospace systems.

Founded in 2021, we operate at the intersection of industrial engineering and technological innovation. We build systems that enable efficient global commerce and contribute to national aerospace capabilities.

Our technical teams execute projects spanning container engineering, mobility logistics systems, and satellite component development. We serve OEMs, shipping enterprises, government agencies, and international clients.
